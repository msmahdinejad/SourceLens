# Optional Charge While You Shop demo papers

This directory contains metadata only. A fresh SourceLens checkout contains no PDFs.
`paperlens demo` explicitly downloads two open-access EV-charging studies, verifies
their pinned SHA-256 checksums from `manifest.json`, and indexes them through the ordinary
workspace ingestion path.

| Paper | Publisher | Reuse terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *Consumer Preferences for Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure Based on the Text Mining Method* | MDPI / Energies | CC BY 4.0 |
| *Transatlantic Technical Recommendations for Government Funded Implementation of Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure* | European Commission Joint Research Centre | CC BY 4.0 |

The papers retain their authors' and publishers' rights and are not redistributed by
SourceLens. Bibliographic details, official companion sources, demo prompts, and media
guidance are documented in [the public demo research note](../docs/demo/source-research.md).
